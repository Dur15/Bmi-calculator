Privacy Policy

Data Collection & Processing

At Harmony BMI Calculator, we take user privacy seriously. All core calculations (BMI, BMR, and Body Fat) are executed locally within your own web browser. We do not transmit, collect, store, or share any personal health metrics or physical characteristics that you enter into our tools.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We partner with Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our web platform. To ensure you understand how your data is handled by our advertising vendors, please note the following requirements:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites across the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted advertisements based on your behavioral visitation histories.
  • You can choose to opt out of highly personalized advertising features at any time by modifying your settings in the Google Ads Settings dashboard.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party cookie usage for targeted web advertising by visiting the About Ads Choice portal.

Log Files & Analytics Tracking

Like standard web servers, this site utilizes log files and anonymous analytic scripts (such as Google Analytics and Ezoic platforms). These log sets include standard diagnostic data such as internet protocol (IP) addresses, generic browser layouts, internet service providers (ISP), referring and exit page workflows, platform variations, and time-stamped interaction data to analyze trends and securely manage server traffic.

GDPR and CCPA Compliance

We strictly respect regional data mandates including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Users have total control over cookie consents via our integrated Consent Management Platform (CMP). If you wish to clear cookies or query server log data, contact our security officer via the channels noted below.
